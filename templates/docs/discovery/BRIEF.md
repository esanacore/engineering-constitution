# Discovery Brief

Status: Draft

## Problem and Evidence

Describe the contributor, current workflow, latest concrete example and source.
Label each entry observed, participant claim, assumption, or unknown. Use
sanitized source references; keep raw interviews outside public repositories.

## Alternatives and Boundaries

Compare doing nothing, a process improvement, and a small software slice.
Record exclusions and constraints with the person who supplied them.

## Decisions and Disagreements

Record the disagreement, alternatives, accountable owner, decision, rationale,
date, and durable reference. No decision exists until the owner makes it.

## Contributor Read-Back

Record whether the contributor identified an assumption, corrected a requirement,
and explained an exclusion. Keep failures and follow-up questions visible.

## Contract

Canonical intent: `../PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`.
Review record: `REVIEW.md`. Pilot record: `PILOT.md`.
